Privacy Policy

ICSC 2027 Conference App

Effective Date: 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the ICSC 2027 Conference App. This application is designed to provide information about the International Conference on Signal Processing and Communication (ICSC 2027), including conference schedules, programmes, speakers, venue information and announcements.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

The application does not require attendees to create an account or log in. It does not directly ask attendees to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address or password.

However, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the services used to operate the application, such as device information, diagnostic data and usage information.

3. Firebase and Data Storage

The application uses Google Firebase services, including Cloud Firestore, to store and retrieve conference-related information such as schedules, programmes and announcements.

Firebase and its underlying infrastructure may process technical information necessary to provide, maintain and secure these services.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through the application may be used to:

  • Display conference schedules and programme details.
  • Provide speaker, venue and event information.
  • Support the My Schedule feature.
  • Maintain application performance and reliability.
  • Identify and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Help protect the application against misuse.

5. My Schedule

The My Schedule feature allows users to save conference sessions for personal reference. These preferences may be stored locally on the user's device.

Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored schedule preferences.

6. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services, including Google Firebase, to support its functionality. These services may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The application may also contain links to external websites, including the official ICSC 2027 conference website. When you open an external website, its own privacy policy and terms apply.

For more information, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Official ICSC 2027 Website

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

Depending on the services and software development kits enabled in the application, analytics or diagnostic information may be processed to understand application performance, identify errors and improve reliability.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. Technical information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase when required to operate, maintain and secure the application.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Conference information is retained as needed to provide the application's features. Locally stored preferences can generally be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the application.

Technical information handled by third-party services may be retained according to the respective service providers' retention policies.

11. Children's Privacy

This application is intended to provide general conference information. It does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, along with an updated effective date.
